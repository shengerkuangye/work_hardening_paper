                                                         TA16 alloy tube with a yield strength of 750 MPa and dimensions of Φ14.4 × 2.15 mm was successfully
Strengthening mechanisms                                 fabricated. Simulation results indicate that a single-pass reduction of 45% yields optimal stress distribution and
                                                         forming quality, effectively preventing surface wrinkling and cracking. Experimental observations reveal that
                                                         with increasing rolling passes, the initial equiaxed grain structure gradually develops into a micron-scale fibrous
                                                         morphology. The average grain size refines from 19.68 μm to 2.52 μm, the fraction of low-angle grain boundaries
                                                         increases to 67.8%, the geometrically necessary dislocation density reaches 2.94 × 1014 m− 2, and the texture
                                                         evolves from an atypical bimodal distribution to a strong {10-10}//AD fiber texture. Concerning deformation
                                                         mechanisms, {10-12} tensile twinning dominates at early stages, whereas basal slip and pyramidal <c+a> slip
                                                         prevail later, with prismatic slip strongly suppressed due to grain rotation. Mechanically, the ultimate tensile
                                                         strength rises to 852 MPa while elongation declines to 9.5%. Among strengthening contributions, grain refine­
                                                         ment plays a more significant role in strength enhancement, acting synergistically with dislocation strengthening
                                                         to dominate the strengthening and toughening behavior of the alloy. This work elucidates the intrinsic proc­
                                                         ess–microstructure–performance relationship during multi-pass cold rolling, providing a theoretical foundation
                                                         for process optimization and precise property control in titanium alloy tubes.
